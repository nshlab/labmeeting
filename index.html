Our theme thie term is foundations of causal machine learning, cutting across modern frameworks from targeted learning (van der Laan and Rubin 2006; Gruber and van der Laan 2010; van der Laan and Rose 2011) to construct asymptotically linear estimators of pathwise differentiable statistical parameters to Neyman orthogonality and double machine learning (Chernozhukov et al. 2018; Foster and Syrgkanis 2023). To deepen our perspective, we will also consider common areas of study between these, including doubly robust inference (Benkeser et al. 2017), the relationship between pathwise differentiability and Neyman orthognality (Chen et al. 2026), and recent advances in the estimation of nuisance functions (McCartan and Huang 2026). As with Spring 2026, we alternate weekly between presentations of the assigned readings and informal research-connection talks covering the relationship between recently discussed topics and ongoing projects in our group.

Time permitting, we may discuss additional topics—listed below—which may replace readings in the above list (pending group discussion) or otherwise serve as potential readings for Spring 2027.

  • Classics in semi-parametric theory: Klaassen (1987), Robins and Rotnitzky (1995)
  • Inference in time-varying, longitudinal settings: Westling et al. (2020), Dı́az et al. (2021), Luedtke et al. (2018), Dı́az et al. (2024), Dı́az et al. (2026), Murphy (2003), Rotnitzky et al. (2017)
  • Function estimation, adaptive inference, and frontier topics: Kennedy (2023), Chernozhukov et al. (2024) Lan and Syrgkanis (2024), van der Laan et al. (2025), Luedtke and Chung (2024), Liu et al. (2017), Liu et al. (2020)
